Our Clients

Our client base includes large and small listed and private companies as well as public sector organisations and parastatals in South Africa and the southern African region.

Our remuneration consulting experience of more than 20 years includes executive remuneration assignments for the advice of the most senior executive(s), the Board and/or the Remuneration Committee, in a variety of organizations in both the Public and the Private Sectors.

Specific examples

  • Grapnel Property Group
    Advice on competitive market remuneration of management and staff members based on role clarification and survey database information.

  • Hospitality Property Fund
    Human Resources due diligence and risk assessments, as well as advice and assistance in developing and implementing human resources strategy.

  • MLC Quantity Surveyors
    Conversion from traditional remuneration packages with benefits added on to cost to company packages; and review and update of terms and conditions of employment of executives and staff.

  • Maluti-a-Phofung Water
    Implementation of job profiling, job evaluation and pay structuring for staff and management.

  • Ellerine Furnishers
    Job demarcation investigation and role clarification with job incumbents and representatives.

  • Anglo Alpha (Holcim)
    Introduction of staff and executive job evaluation systems, remuneration system development/design; and performance based pay for executives and management.

  • Credit Guarantee Insurance Corporation
    Review of the job grades and guaranteed packages of senior executives.

  • Impala Platinum
    Review of executive remuneration and rewards; and advice to Remuneration Committee.

  • Vodacom Group
    Job grading and remuneration reviews for senior executives and management.

  • SA Blood Transfusion Service
    Top strategy and target-setting sessions with CEO and executives.

  • Mines Pension Funds (MPF (Pty) Limited)
    Development of executive incentive scheme.

  • Universities of Pretoria, Free State and KZN
    Job evaluation and grading; review of executive remuneration; and development of a performance management system.

  • SwaziBank
    Review of the remuneration, performance contract and performance incentives of the Managing Director; formulation of recommendations for change; and their justification at Board level.

  • Swaziland Railway
    Job evaluation and grading, together with remuneration structuring.

  • Swaziland Posts and Telecommunications Corporation Limited
    Job evaluation system training, job grading and organisational restructuring.

  • Daimler Chrysler, SA
    Job evaluation system training and job profiling and grading.

  • CEF (Pty) Limited
    Development/design of performance management and reward systems.
