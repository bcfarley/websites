Mike Honnet Consulting

Organisations of all kinds depend for their success on the commitment, skills and abilities of their workforces. We have found that one of the most effective ways to drive productivity and generate growth and profitability is to create closer alignment between people strategies and business strategies.

Our solutions Get in touch

We partner with clients to help them develop performance and pay solutions consistent with their HR strategy and changing business needs. Our years of experience combined with the research and technology of our global associates enables us to provide your company with the solutions you need for a range of performance and pay requirements.

Our solutions are designed to assist clients to:

  1. Develop a strategic view of pay as a means of achieving corporate goals.
  2. Create custom-built systems that encourage productivity and high performance.
  3. Focus people on outcomes they can influence.
  4. Reward people when goals are achieved.
  5. Transfer expertise and skills to key people.

It is our emphasis on custom-building unique solutions, together with skill transfer and employee participation, that enables us to ensure buy-in and support.
